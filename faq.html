Septic Tank Pumping & Service FAQ — Prince Albert

How often should a septic tank be pumped?

Many households pump every 2–4 years, but it depends on tank size, household size and water use. A local pro can recommend a schedule.

What are signs my tank needs service?

Slow drains, gurgling pipes, sewage odours, wet or extra-green spots over the field, or backups.

Do you service acreages and cabins?

Yes — requests from acreages, farms and lake properties around Prince Albert are welcome.
